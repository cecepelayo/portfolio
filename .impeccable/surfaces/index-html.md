# Surface: index.html (one-page portfolio)

Mode: Experience. Audience: small product brands, publishers/cultural institutions, studios/agencies, recruiters. Job: judge Cecilia's work in seconds and write to her. Action: contact (email/social), always one click away via "Hablemos". Proof: six real projects and two personal paintings; nothing invented. Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, ES/EN on one page, logo used as is, honest context labels, optimised images only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a matchbox collector's display board: each project is a numbered matchbox label in a series, Cecilia's name is the series' master label in the centre. Refuses the category default of a hero headline over a uniform thumbnail grid.

OWN-WORLD: Bright off-white board ground; flat vivid inks (vermilion, cornflower blue, leaf green, pink) over ink black, one ink per discipline; logo turquoise reserved by law for actions. Labels: printed card with thick black keyline and inner rule, ornamental frames, serial "Nº 0X" in mono. Display in high-contrast italic serif (Bodoni Moda) in two inks; label text in a grotesque; serials in DM Mono. Ornamental bands (cenefas) between passages, built from her motifs (placeholder fleurons until her PNG cutouts arrive). Composition only at 0/90°.

STORY: Visitor sees a collection of real finished work and a person who draws it herself; understands what she can be hired for; writes to her.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Thin bar: logo left, ES/EN and turquoise "Hablemos" right. Centre: master label with ornate frame, "Cecilia Pelayo" huge italic serif in two inks, the CECE logo overlapping the name, line "Dibujo yo. Y lo convierto en producto." Around it, the six matchbox labels with cover images, Nº, discipline and context printed on their face; Cata la Lata is the large box. Signature interaction: clicking a box slides its drawer out, then the gallery opens; hovering one box drops ink on the others.

FORM: Fosforera (Spanish matchbox labels, collector series), position 5 of 7 on the grounded list, raised by declined challengers (palette law, fixed serials, one ink per discipline, handle-the-object drawer, context on the face, single focus).
